# ADR 0007 — Customer cancellation and independent MOCK refunds

Implement full-booking cancellation before first departure for confirmed MOCK flights. Preserve every accepted quote. Legacy unstructured non-refundable wording leaves tax entitlement ambiguous, so it is unsupported for automatic cancellation/refund; fail closed instead of inventing entitlement. Newly searched fixtures explicitly offer full-total-refund or zero-total-refund policies. These are synthetic policies, not legal/tax guidance or real supplier commitments.

Create an owned immutable cancellation quote, version 1, expiring after five minutes or departure. Customer explicitly accepts its refund/retained amounts. One durable cancellation case per booking and principal/operation-scoped keys prevent duplicate effects. Cancellation and refund states remain independent. Initial checkout remains historical. V3 detail/list reads explicitly include current aftercare; legacy v2 reads fail closed with 409 when a cancellation case exists, preserving old schemas and avoiding stale confirmation displays. Document access is blocked while cancellation is pending, cancelled or unresolved.

Booking owns cancellation orchestration and its supplier adapter; Payments owns pending refund commitments, immutable final journal adjustments and refund settlement. Communication uses published core ports, preserving inward dependencies. Do not update/delete capture entries. Serialize refund entitlement against the captured account, reserve pending amounts, and reject sums above captured funds. Positive refund completion appends balanced reverse entries. Zero entitlement becomes NOT_DUE, never a fabricated successful refund.

Persist claim/attempt/operation identity before effects. Query stable operation IDs before safely idempotent mutations. Provider cancellation commits its own receipt; unknown replies reconcile before any refund. Refund submission is durable PENDING, with a separate MOCK provider settler; cancellation can be CANCELLED while refund remains PENDING. Lost responses/process death recover without another cancellation or refund. Five bounded attempts per step lead to REVIEW rather than invented finality. New outboxes are atomic but dispatch remains separate work.

MOCK scenarios: success, lost cancellation response, lost refund response, and delayed refund. No gateway/webhook or real financial effect is enabled. Partial passenger/segment cancellation, fees varying after acceptance, real provider access, dispute/settlement tooling, customer retries after a rejected case, and audited REVIEW resolution remain explicitly incomplete.

Acceptance AC-031–035: immutable policy/quotation and ownership; concurrent idempotent acceptance; cancel-before-refund; pending/refund integrity and over-refund prevention; actual process recovery, expiry, denied access, document gating and responsive UI.
